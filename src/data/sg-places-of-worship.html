Singapore Worship Map

Areas with more places of worship per resident have more residents with no religion. Old central districts hold many temples, mosques and churches that serve the whole island; the new towns house far more people around a few planned sites. Click an area or a dot for detail.

Planning areas ranked

What counts as a site. A site is land zoned “Place of Worship” in the URA Master Plan 2025; touching parcels are merged into one. Religion and names come from OpenStreetMap entries within 25 m. Sites with no entry nearby are marked Unmatched and may be land not yet built on.

Places outside zoned land are OpenStreetMap places of worship in shophouses, commercial buildings and other land uses. They are not in the per-resident rates.

Population is residents aged 15 and over from the Census of Population 2020, which reports 30 planning areas by name. Other areas have no figure. Chinese temples are tagged Buddhist or Taoist inconsistently in OpenStreetMap.

Sources: Master Plan 2025 Land Use, Census 2020 religion by planning area, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Data downloaded .
